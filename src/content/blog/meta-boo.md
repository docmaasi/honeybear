A **meta boo** is a romantic partner you know entirely inside a virtual world. You met in a headset. You spend evenings together in a room that does not physically exist. You may have never seen their face, and you might not know their surname.

People outside it tend to hear that and file it under "not a real relationship." People inside it know better, and usually cannot explain why.

## Why the feeling is not a trick

Attachment does not form through touch. It forms through **attention, repetition, and disclosure** — being paid attention to, showing up again, and telling someone things you do not tell everyone.

A virtual world delivers all three, often more concentrated than daily life does. There is no commute, no scheduling, no getting dressed. There is just the two of you in a room, most nights, talking. Many people find they disclose faster in VR than they ever did across a dinner table, because the avatar takes some of the exposure off them.

So the bond is not a simulation of a bond. It is the same mechanism, running in a different place.

## What the format genuinely hides

Being honest about this matters more than defending it.

- **You are seeing an edited person.** Not necessarily a liar — everyone in VR is somewhat curated. But you are meeting them at their most composed, in a setting they control.
- **You have not seen them tired, ill, late, or dealing with their mother.** Those are not small omissions. A great deal of compatibility lives there.
- **Friction is optional.** You can log off. Real proximity does not offer that, and learning to stay in the room is most of what long relationships are made of.

None of that makes the connection fake. It makes it **incomplete** — which is a different word, and a fairer one.

## The question worth asking

Not *is this real?* It is real. The useful question is:

> **What do I still not know about this person, and am I avoiding finding out?**

If the answer is "quite a lot, and yes, a bit" — that is worth sitting with. Not as an accusation. As information.

## When it gets complicated

Two situations come up constantly on the show.

The first is a meta boo alongside a real-world partner who does not know. That is its own subject, and we cover it in [married in the real world, involved in the digital one](/blog/married-digital).

The second is a meta boo who begins to control — who needs to know where you are, who you are talking to, which worlds you visit. Control travels perfectly well through a headset. If any of that is familiar, [digital abuse](/blog/digital-abuse) is worth reading.

## The short version

A meta boo is a real relationship with real feeling and a genuine gap in it. Both halves of that sentence are true, and the people who do best are the ones who hold both.
